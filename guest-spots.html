Massage for events, festivals and workplaces

I also work on site — bringing the couch, or a chair, to wherever people already are. Bristol and Bath as standard, further afield for a full day or a weekend.

Festivals and events

Treatments through the day for people who have been on their feet since morning. Chair massage where space is tight, couch work where there is room for it.

Workplaces and corporate days

Chair massage at desks, or a quiet room set aside for the day. Shoulders, necks and forearms, mostly — the parts a working week reaches first.

Retreats and private groups

A day or a weekend alongside yoga, walking, or whatever the retreat is built around. Back-to-back treatments, booked by whoever is organising.
