How home visits work →

Which one should I book?

If you have a specific area that aches, or you train hard, book deep tissue.

If you are stressed, not sleeping, or just need to stop, book relaxation.

If you are unsure, book either and tell me on the day. I tailor the treatment once we have talked, and there is no price difference between them — the labels are mostly there to help you choose.

Sixty or ninety?

Ninety, if you can.

Sixty is a proper treatment, but it means choosing where to focus — your back or your hips, your shoulders or your neck. Ninety means we do all of it, and it gives you long enough to genuinely switch off rather than just beginning to.

Most people who try a ninety don't go back to sixty.

Good to know

What happens in the first appointment?

We spend ten minutes talking before anything else — a short health questionnaire, then a conversation about what you want from the hour. Then you undress to your underwear, get under the towels, and I leave the room while you do. You will be covered throughout, with only the area I am working on uncovered.

Will it hurt?

It shouldn't. Deep tissue means slow and specific, not brutal. There can be a strong sensation when we find something that has been holding for a while, but it should stay in the range where you can breathe and stay relaxed. I check in as we go, and you can ask me to ease off at any point.

Should I book if I'm pregnant, unwell, or on medication?

Often yes, but talk to me first. There are some conditions where massage needs adapting and a few where it isn't appropriate. Tell me what's going on when you book and I will be straight with you about whether it's a good idea.

How often should I come?

Depends entirely on what you're dealing with. Someone managing a long-standing problem might come every three or four weeks; someone using it to stay level might come every couple of months. I'll give you an honest view at the end of your first treatment, and I won't push you towards more than you need.

Cancellations

Treatments are paid for when you book, and cancelling refunds you in full, straight back to your card. Please give 24 hours' notice where you can, so the slot can go to someone else. Life happens, and I'm reasonable about it.
