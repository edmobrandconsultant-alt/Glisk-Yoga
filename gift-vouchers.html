How it works

  1. Choose an amount and pay by card. Takes about a minute. You do not need an account.
  2. The voucher arrives by email, straight away. Even at nine o'clock on Christmas Eve. Print it, put it in a card, or forward the email — whichever suits.
  3. They get in touch and we find a time. They can book online or call me. If they would rather I came to them, we sort that out then.
  4. They have a year. Twelve months from the date you buy it. And if they overrun that by a few weeks, talk to me — I would rather they used it.

Buying for a parent or grandparent

This is the most common reason people buy vouchers from me, and it is worth saying plainly: I can come to them.

A home visit removes everything that stops an older person actually using a gift like this — the parking, the stairs, the unfamiliar building, getting cold on the walk back to the car. I bring the couch, the towels and the warmth, and they get up afterwards and are already home.

If that is what you have in mind, just say so when you buy and I will note it on the voucher. Home visits are for women only — the reasoning is on the home visits page.

Buying for someone having a hard time

A massage is one of the few gifts that asks nothing of the person receiving it. No effort, no reciprocation, no having to be cheerful about it.

If you are buying for someone who is grieving, recovering, exhausted or newly a parent, tell me when you buy. I will keep the voucher open-ended rather than pushing them to book, and I will work gently the first time unless they ask otherwise.

People sometimes worry that a massage is the wrong gift when someone is unwell. It usually isn't — but do check with them or their medical team if there is active treatment going on, and I am happy to talk it through first.

The small print, in plain English

How long is it valid?

Twelve months from the date of purchase, and the date is printed on the voucher. If someone comes to me a bit after that, I would almost always rather honour it than have it wasted — just ask.

Can it be part-used?

Yes. If the voucher is worth less than the treatment, they pay the difference on the day. If it is worth more, the balance stays on the voucher for the rest of the twelve months.

Can I get a refund?

Vouchers are non-refundable and can't be exchanged for cash. But they are transferable — if the person you bought it for can't use it, they can pass it on to someone else. Just let me know the new name.

What if they've never had a massage before?

Then it is a particularly good present. I will talk them through everything at the start, and there is nothing they need to know or do beforehand. Plenty of my clients came in through a voucher having never booked a massage in their lives.

Do you do vouchers for a business?

Yes — if you want to thank a team, I can do a batch. Worth knowing that UK employers can give staff a non-cash gift of up to £50 free of tax and National Insurance under HMRC's trivial benefits rules, and a massage voucher qualifies where a cash voucher would not. Get in touch and I will sort out the paperwork side.

Can I have a physical one to hand over?

The emailed voucher is designed to be printed and it looks good on ordinary paper. If you would rather have a proper card posted to you, ask at least a week ahead and I will put one in the post.
